## Addressing Inappropriate Behavior
We expect every team member to contribute to a friendly and respectful environment. Acts like bullying, harassment, victimization, or discrimination will **not be tolerated**.

- If you experience harassment, feel free to contact your PI
- If you notice someone else facing inappropriate behavior, you can talk to them privately or report it to your PI
- You are **not obliged** to manage these situations on your own — we have resources and methods available to help you

### Personal Hygiene & Attire
We ask all lab members to maintain basic standards of personal hygiene and to dress in a manner appropriate for a professional academic environment.
