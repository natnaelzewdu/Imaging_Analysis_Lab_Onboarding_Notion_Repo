## Socializing
Building a strong group dynamic involves getting to know one another. Team members are encouraged to have lunch together during work hours or socialize outside of work.

### Lab Gatherings
The lab holds large full-lab gatherings approximately twice a year — typically around **Thanksgiving** and during the **Summer**. These are great opportunities to connect with everyone in a relaxed setting.

![Lab summer outing at the lake](local:data/Onboarding Resources/lab_photos/lab_photo.jpg)

### Other Opportunities
- Weekly or informal group lunches — check with current lab members for the schedule
- Local activities and social events — ask around for what’s currently happening
