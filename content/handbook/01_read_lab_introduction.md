## Welcome to Imaging-Analysis Lab!

We are a team of researchers based at the [Translational Research in Neuroimaging and Data Science (TReNDS) Center](https://trendscenter.org/) at [Georgia State University](https://www.gsu.edu()). Our work centers on **neuroimaging and machine learning** — developing and applying methods such as independent component analysis (ICA) to study brain networks and functional connectivity, including how they differ in conditions like schizophrenia. This document provides information to help all new members of the Imaging-Analysis Lab, and the wider research community, understand how our group operates. Here, you will find detailed information about life as a group member, what we expect from our researchers, and the support we can offer to your research development.

We hope this document serves as a useful resource throughout your time at Imaging-Analysis Lab. We expect new members to read this document by the end of their first month of joining our group. If you have any questions or concerns, your PI or other members will be happy to discuss them. We welcome feedback that you may have on this document, and how we can continue to improve researcher experience across the academic spectrum.
