## Postdoctoral Researchers (Postdocs)
Postdocs are researchers who have recently completed their Ph.D. studies and are working in the lab to gain additional research experience. Their contributions include:

- Contributing to ongoing projects and collaborating with students
- Mentoring and sometimes supervising Master and PhD students
- Taking an active role in writing project definitions and outlines, ensuring projects are successful from start to finish
- Assisting in writing grants and funding applications
- Because postdoc candidates already have their doctoral credentials, they are able to teach informally for colleges or other educational facilities
