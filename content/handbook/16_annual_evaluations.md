## Annual Evaluations
Most departments and funding sources require students and faculty to complete annual evaluations.

- Keep your **CV and IDP updated** at all times
- Be prepared to report on your progress each year (usually towards the end of the Spring Semester)

### What to include in your annual report
Your annual report should cover the period January–December and typically includes:

- **Reflection questions**: strengths, challenges, areas for improvement, goals for the coming year, support needed
- **Publications**: list all manuscripts (first-author and co-author) as *Published*, *Under Review*, or *In Preparation*. Include full citation with DOI where available.
- **Conference papers & abstracts**: list all with venue and publication status
- **Internal research events**: lab talks, symposia, retreat presentations
- **Awards**: both awarded and applied-but-not-received
- **Scientific service**: reviewing, panels, outreach
- **Mentoring**: any students you supported
- **Other contributions**: grant assistance, data curation, training others
