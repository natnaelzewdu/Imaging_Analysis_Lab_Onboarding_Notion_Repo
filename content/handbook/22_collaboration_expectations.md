## Setting Collaboration Expectations
It is important to be clear and up front about what each member expects to contribute and be credited for in a given collaboration.

### Authorship
- Have important details such as **authorship agreed upon and documented in writing early on**, especially when you expect to make significant contributions of time and resources
- This will help avoid potential misunderstanding and conflict later
- Discuss authorship with your advisor or the PI of a given project as **norms may differ**
