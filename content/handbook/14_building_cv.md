## Building Your CV
It is highly encouraged to devote time towards developing skills and experience relevant to your individual career trajectory.

### Publications
- Publications are a key metric used to evaluate professional researchers in academia as well as industry
- **PhD students should aim to have 5 published peer-reviewed journal articles by graduation**, ideally with 2 per year in the last 2 years of their training

### Other skills to develop
In addition to your primary research tasks, seek to develop a variety of other skills relevant to your professional development:
- Teaching and mentoring opportunities
- Public outreach (field-relevant service activities)
- Serving on committees and in leadership roles
