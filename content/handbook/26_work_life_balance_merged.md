## Work-Life Balance & Wellbeing

### Balancing Working Hours
In general, we do **not** enforce specific working hours. While spending time in the lab can be beneficial, group members are not obligated to be there the entire day.

- If you are a **new member**, we encourage you to work from the lab at least three times a week — this way the onboarding process will go smoother
- Researchers should devise a schedule that suits them, talk to their PI about it, and address any challenges hindering productivity
- You should **not** feel pressured to respond to emails or messages beyond your work hours
- Respect your colleagues' working hours
- **Important**: Armin requests that you send him your planned work hours, indicating when you will be on campus, so that he can plan around that. Consider sharing a Google Calendar with your schedule.

### Work and Personal Life
Maintaining a healthy work-life balance helps manage the pressures of academic research. This involves allocating time for activities you enjoy and personal obligations.

- Try to set reasonable daily working hours, and times completely disconnected from work (including work-related emails)
- While the timing of your holidays is typically flexible, you might need to consider other commitments or deadlines

### Prioritizing Mental Wellbeing
Allocating time to relax away from work significantly contributes to your mental wellbeing.
