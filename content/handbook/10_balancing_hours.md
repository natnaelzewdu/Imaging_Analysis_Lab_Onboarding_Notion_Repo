## Balancing Working Hours
In general, we do **not** enforce specific working hours. While spending time in the lab can be beneficial, group members are not obligated to be there the entire day.

- If you are a **new member**, we encourage you to work from the lab at least three times a week — this way the onboarding process will go smoother
- Researchers should devise a schedule that suits them, talk to their PI about it, and address any challenges hindering productivity
- You should **not** feel pressured to respond to emails or messages beyond your work hours
- Respect your colleagues' working hours
