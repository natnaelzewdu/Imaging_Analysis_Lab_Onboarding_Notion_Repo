## Conference Expectations & Logistics

### Funding
- There are often funds available to help students with conference costs through **travel awards** from their department or university (e.g., The Dean's Graduate Research Grant)
- Discuss with your advisor whether grant funds may be available for covering travel expenses
- If traveling for a conference may be a financial burden, is unsafe, or you are unable to attend for other reasons, discuss this with your advisor
- As an alternative, you may be able to arrange for others in your lab to present your work (especially for large conferences like OHBM)

### Travel paperwork
- Complete the **Travel Authorization form** prior to your trip
- Submit your **Travel Expense Statement** within 10 days of your return
- More detailed instructions and reimbursement forms can be found on the TReNDS intranet

### Accessing the TReNDS Intranet
The intranet contains up-to-date travel policies, reimbursement forms, and other internal resources.
- **Intranet access**: [trendscenter.org/intranet-access/](https://trendscenter.org/intranet-access/)
- To open intranet links, right-click the link and choose **"Open link as [your GSU account]"**, then sign in.
- **Room reservations**: [TReNDS Center intranet — Room reservation](https://sites.google.com/gsu.edu/trends-center-intranet/home)
