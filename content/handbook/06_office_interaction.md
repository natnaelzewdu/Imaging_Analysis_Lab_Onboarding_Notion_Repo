## Office Interaction Guidelines
A portion of our workstations are located within "cubicle" offices, apart from the designated room offices. It is important to exhibit consideration for your fellow colleagues who might possess distinct needs or preferences.

- Adhere to fundamental open plan etiquette — refrain from causing disruptive noises or encroaching on neighboring spaces
- For discussions that are extended or might create disturbances, use allocated meeting rooms or common spaces

### Scheduling Meeting Rooms
- Book rooms through the [TReNDS Center intranet — Room reservation](https://sites.google.com/gsu.edu/trends-center-intranet/home)
- To access intranet links, right-click and choose **"Open link as [your GSU account]"**, then sign in
