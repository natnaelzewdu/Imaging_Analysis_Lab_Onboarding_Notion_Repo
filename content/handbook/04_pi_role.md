## Principal Investigator (PI)
The PI is typically a faculty member, often a professor, who leads and oversees the research activities in the lab. Key responsibilities include:

- Offering strategic direction to the lab's research focus
- Mentoring and guiding students and lab members
- Securing research funding
- Fostering collaborations
- Managing projects
- Contributing to education
- Publishing research
- Overseeing administrative tasks

The PI's comprehensive contributions play a pivotal role in advancing research, nurturing talent, and enriching the academic community.
