## Individual Development Plan (IDP)
Students should create (and regularly update) an Individual Development Plan (IDP). An IDP provides an outline of a student's progress toward their degree and includes:

- A timeline for completing required coursework and milestones
- Current and planned research projects which will enable you to achieve your overarching goals for education and career
- Skills you want to develop during your training

It is recommended that you dedicate **a few hours a week** — separate from your lab-related tasks — toward your individual research interests and professional development.
### Meeting with Your Advisor
A key part of the IDP is meeting with and communicating with your advisor about key milestones and your graduation timeline. Plan to meet with your advisor **at least once per semester** specifically to review your IDP — making sure you are meeting deadlines and having the chance to discuss and address any barriers.

For IDP templates and guidance, see [myIDP (Science Careers)](https://myidp.sciencecareers.org/) — a free online tool widely used by graduate students and postdocs in the sciences.
