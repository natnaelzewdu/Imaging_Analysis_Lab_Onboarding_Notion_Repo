## Research Conduct & Ethics
We are committed to ethical and responsible research conduct.

### CITI Training Requirement
All lab members are strongly encouraged (and in some departments, required) to complete **CITI training** and maintain an active certification. This training covers responsible conduct of research, human subjects protections, and research ethics.
- [CITI Program — Collaborative Institutional Training Initiative](https://about.citiprogram.org/)

### What to do if you have concerns
- Talk to your PI if you are unsure of the ethical implications of any given action
- Talk to your PI if you feel pressure to engage in ethically compromising behavior
- Talk to your PI if you observe research misconduct happening around you
- If you feel you are being asked to engage in practices that you are uncomfortable with, it is always best to **respectfully raise your concerns** in the first instance

### Escalation path
If you feel your concerns are not being given serious consideration:
- Talk to your PI
- A secondary advisor
- Your department head
- A trained harassment advisor
- If the issue cannot be resolved locally, refer to University guidelines for reporting research misconduct
