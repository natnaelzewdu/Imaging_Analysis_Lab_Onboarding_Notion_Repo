## Reproducible Research
Make regular efforts to keep your data, code, and other research materials organized.

### Best practices
- Maintain a **"vault"** for each project you work on, including:
  - Documentation of the filepaths where data and code are stored/archived
  - An outline of study methods and procedures
  - Relevant publications or other critical information
- Strive to communicate clearly to help others avoid mistakes
- The goal is to facilitate reproducibility by ensuring that you and others can find needed information in a timely manner

### Tools for reproducibility
- [Git / GitHub](https://github.com/) for version control
- [conda environment exports](https://docs.conda.io/projects/conda/en/latest/user-guide/tasks/manage-environments.html) for Python reproducibility
- [OSF](https://osf.io/) for project management and pre-registration
