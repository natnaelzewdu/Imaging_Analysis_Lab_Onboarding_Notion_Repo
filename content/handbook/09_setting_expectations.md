## Setting Clear Expectations
A crucial aspect of career satisfaction involves having well-defined and agreed-upon expectations. Yet, it is often challenging to predict the time required for a specific task.

- While it is important to establish project timelines, remaining **adaptable** and understanding if things take longer is key
- **Communication** is vital for managing expectations and defining work boundaries
- If your workload affects your wellbeing, consider discussing it with your PI or line manager
