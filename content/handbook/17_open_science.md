## Open Science
Open science practices facilitate reproducible research and accountability and help move the field forward by making research findings and opportunities more widely accessible.

### What's expected
- Many funding agencies and journals encourage (and may even require) open science practices such as making data and code available via online repositories
- Uploading manuscripts to pre-print servers such as [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/) prior to research conferences has the benefit of helping to publicize and disseminate your research (by sharing QR codes and links) and facilitate collaboration efforts

- [Open Science Framework (OSF)](https://osf.io/)
