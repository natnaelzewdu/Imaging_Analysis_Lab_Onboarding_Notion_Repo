## Equality, Diversity & Inclusion
At our lab, we are fully committed to fostering an environment of Equality, Diversity & Inclusion (EDI). We embrace the diverse backgrounds of our lab members and promote equal opportunities for all. By cultivating a culture of respect and inclusivity, we aim to create a space where every individual feels a sense of belonging.

### Our Commitment
- We value the unique perspectives that each lab member brings based on their background, culture, and experiences
- We strive to ensure equitable access to opportunities, mentorship, and resources for all lab members
- Discriminatory behavior of any kind is not tolerated

### What You Can Do
- Be mindful of inclusive language and behavior in meetings and communications
- Speak up if you witness exclusionary behavior
- Participate in university EDI training when available
