## Why collaborate?
The [TReNDS center](https://trendscenter.org/) is built on collaboration. The research in our lab is very interdisciplinary in nature.

### Benefits
- Collaboration enables us to work with researchers with **complementary expertise**
- Working with others who share common expertise lets you accomplish greater tasks and exchange insight or innovative ideas
- **Co-authorship** often results from collaboration, which can indicate to future employers your willingness and effectiveness when working as part of a team

### Important considerations
- Collaboration is a commitment of time and resources
- Data sharing requires certain permissions
- **Always discuss with your advisor/PI first** before entering a collaboration
