## Conferences Overview
Conferences are a great opportunity to learn and increase your awareness of research in your field. They also provide opportunities to disseminate your own work and network with other researchers.

### Tips
- Discuss with your advisor which conferences you should aim to attend
- Be mindful of **abstract submission deadlines**
- Complete abstract drafts early so you can share them with your advisor and implement their feedback prior to submission
- When abstract submission deadlines are close, your advisor will likely have limited time (they are probably working on multiple abstracts)
