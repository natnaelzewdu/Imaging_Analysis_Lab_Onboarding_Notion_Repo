## Students in Imaging-Analysis Lab
Students in Imaging-Analysis Lab encompass both Graduate Students (Master's and Ph.D.) and Undergraduate Researchers. Graduate students play a significant role, conducting research for their theses under PI and postdoc guidance. Undergraduates contribute to various lab tasks, gaining hands-on experience.

### Day-to-day
- Focused research work, coding, experiment design, data processing and analysis, and iterative development based on findings
- Attendance at supervisions, engaging in one-on-one collaborations
- Active participation in lab meetings
- Attending educational events like seminars and workshops
- Documentation of research processes and results, aiding potential future publications
- Continuous learning through literature review and engagement with industry resources
- Regular updates to the PI for progress tracking

### What senior members expect from you
- Active involvement in lab activities, discussions, and project collaborations
- Regular updates on research progress, challenges, and achievements through supervisory meetings and lab gatherings
- High-quality research with critical thinking and innovation
- A desire for learning, skill development, openness to feedback, and dedication to personal and academic growth

### Support you can expect
- Guidance and mentorship from senior members — solutions to challenges, refining research skills, constructive feedback
- Networking and collaboration opportunities, broader exposure
- Access to crucial resources like literature and datasets
- Advice on professional development including career growth, conference participation, and presentation skills
- An inclusive and supportive environment where you can comfortably seek help, ask questions, and discuss challenges

### Common challenges
- Research projects can be intricate, particularly when confronted with technical or theoretical challenges
- Effective time management when juggling research, coursework, and other commitments
- Overcoming setbacks and uncertainties in research requires emotional and intellectual resilience
- Developing communication, collaboration, and project management skills can pose a learning curve
- Maintaining work-life balance while dedicating substantial effort to research
