## Conduct in Meetings

We conduct group meetings weekly throughout the academic year. **Attendance is required** unless prior arrangements are made — notify the PI when you will be unable to attend.

Each member is responsible for:

- Sharing their progress with peers and the PI

- Posing questions when needed

- Presenting research papers within relevant domains during meetings

### Weekly Updates

- Come prepared with a brief summary of your progress since the last meeting

- Focus on items appropriate for a group setting — be mindful of everyone's time

- Save detailed individual discussions for one-on-one meetings with the PI
