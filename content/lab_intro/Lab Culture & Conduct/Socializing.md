## Socializing in the Lab

Research is a marathon, not a sprint. The Imaging-Analysis Lab makes a point of coming together outside of work to recharge, celebrate wins, and simply enjoy each other's company. These moments are not extras — they are part of what makes the lab a place people want to stay and do their best work.

## When we get together

The lab gathers socially throughout the year, including:

- **End-of-semester celebrations** — marking the close of a productive semester and recognizing everyone's contributions
- **Holiday and Thanksgiving gatherings** — sharing a meal and taking a break from the grind
- **Conference and award celebrations** — when a lab member presents a paper, wins an award, or defends a thesis, we celebrate together
- **Informal lunches and coffee runs** — spontaneous, low-key catch-ups that keep the day-to-day energy up

## Why it matters

A lab where people know each other beyond their research roles is a lab where collaboration comes naturally, feedback feels safe, and setbacks are easier to weather. Socializing resets momentum, strengthens friendships, and reminds everyone that the people around them are invested in their success — not just the science.

If you have an idea for a lab outing or want to organise something, bring it up at a lab meeting. Everyone is welcome to take the lead.
