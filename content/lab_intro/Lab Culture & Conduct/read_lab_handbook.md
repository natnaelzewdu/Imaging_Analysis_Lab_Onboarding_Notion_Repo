## Read the Lab Handbook

The **Imaging-Analysis Lab Handbook** is the primary reference for lab culture, expectations, and policies. Read it fully during your first month.

> **Access requires a GSU account.** Open the link while signed in to your GSU Microsoft account.

📄 [Imaging-Analysis Lab Handbook (OneDrive)](https://studentgsu-my.sharepoint.com/:w:/g/personal/nalemayehu3_student_gsu_edu/IQDsNTLDixEcSpdu0UwS2eIlAQgqAToVnYuq9rNnHfi_7Zg?e=sfeJsJ)

## Topics covered

### Lab Orientation

- Who is in the lab: students, postdocs, and the PI

- Student roles and day-to-day expectations

- Postdoc roles and responsibilities

- PI role and what to expect from your advisor

### Workplace Conduct

- How to behave in meetings and shared office spaces

- Socializing and building a healthy group dynamic

- Inappropriate behavior policy and reporting resources

### Work-Life Balance & Wellbeing

- Setting clear expectations around hours and workload

- Balancing research with personal wellbeing

- Equality, diversity, and inclusion commitment

### Professional Development

- Individual Development Plan (IDP) — what it is and how to maintain one

- Annual evaluations — format, timeline, and what to include

### Research Practices

- Reproducible research and open science

- Research conduct and ethics

### Collaboration & Conferences

- Collaboration norms and authorship expectations

- Poster printing procedures

- Conference attendance, travel paperwork, and reimbursement
