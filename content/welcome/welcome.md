## Welcome to the Image-Analysis Lab at the TReNDS Center

This document provides information about how the Imaging-Analysis Lab operates and what to expect as a new member. It was written to ensure that all new members of the lab and the wider research community have an insight into how our group works. Here you will find detailed information about life as a group member, what we expect from our researchers, and the support we can offer to your research development.

We hope this document serves as a useful resource throughout your time at the Imaging-Analysis Lab. We expect new members to read this document by the end of their first month of joining our group. If you have any questions or concerns, your PI or other members will be happy to discuss them. We welcome feedback on how we can continue to improve researcher experience across the academic spectrum.

## What is in this onboarding template

- **Lab Intro** - Lab culture, the handbook, and meeting expectations. Start here.
- **Technical Onboarding** - Core neuroimaging concepts, computing environment setup, and hands-on analysis workflows.
- **Tools & Workflows** - Version control, VS Code, and the tools used every day in the lab.
- **Funding & Fellowships** - Grants, fellowships, and travel awards available to lab members.
- **Projects** - Capstone projects and research assignments.

Complete **Lab Intro** first, then move on to **Technical Onboarding**. Within Technical Onboarding, finish all Theory tasks before the Hands-On tasks.
