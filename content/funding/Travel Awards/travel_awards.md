## Travel Awards Overview

Conferences are an important part of your scientific training. Funding is often available — apply early.

### University-level

- **[Dean's Graduate Research Grant](https://graduate.gsu.edu/current-students/funding/)** — GSU award for conference travel. Apply before your travel date.

- **GSU Graduate Student Association** — check for travel funding each semester

### Conference-specific awards

- **[OHBM Merit Abstract Award](https://www.humanbrainmapping.org/)** — travel support for accepted abstracts

- **[ISMRM Educational Stipend](https://www.ismrm.org/)** — for early-career researchers

- Many conferences offer student/trainee travel grants with abstract submission — always check

### Lab grant funds

- Conference travel may be covered by lab grants — discuss with your PI before booking

- Complete the **Travel Authorization form** before your trip and **Travel Expense Statement** within 10 days of return (forms on [TReNDS intranet](https://trendscenter.org/intranet-access/))
