## Grants Overview

The lab is funded by grants from federal agencies including **NIH** and **NSF**. Ask your PI about the specific grants currently active and how your project relates to them.

### How students contribute

- Students may be asked to assist with grant proposals — this is excellent training and should be treated as a priority task

- Contributing to grants counts toward your annual report under "Other Notable Contributions"

### Key funding agencies

- [NIH (National Institutes of Health)](https://www.nih.gov/) — primary funder of biomedical research

- [NSF (National Science Foundation)](https://www.nsf.gov/) — funds computational/data science work

- Discuss with your PI about which grants are currently active and what opportunities exist for you
