## Fellowships Overview

Fellowships provide independent funding and significantly strengthen your CV. Discuss fellowship opportunities with your PI early — many require advance planning and PI support letters.

### Key fellowships for PhD students

- **[NSF GRFP](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/)** — National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship. Apply in your 1st or 2nd year. Provides 3 years of stipend + tuition.

- **[NIH F31 (NRSA)](https://researchtraining.nih.gov/programs/fellowships/F31)** — Individual predoctoral fellowship. Requires an NIH-funded mentor.

- **GSU Dissertation Grant** — internal GSU award; check the [GSU Graduate Studies](https://graduate.gsu.edu/) site for current cycles

### For postdocs

- **[NIH F32 (NRSA)](https://researchtraining.nih.gov/programs/fellowships/F32)** — Postdoctoral fellowship

- **[NIH K99/R00](https://researchtraining.nih.gov/programs/career-development/K99-R00)** — Pathway to Independence award (late postdoc)

> Ask senior lab members about their application experiences. Past applications can be excellent models.
